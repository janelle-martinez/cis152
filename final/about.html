About Pacific Trails Resort

Our Story

At Pacific Trails Resort we blend comfort and nature for a relaxing getaway. Located on the beautiful California coast, our resort gives guests a chance to step away from their busy lives, enjoy the ocean views, and make memories surrounded by nature.

Our Mission

Our mission to provide our guests a comfortable and memorable experience that helps them connect with the outdoors. We want each guest to feel welcome, to enjoy their stay, and to discover what makes the California coast so special.

Discover Our Resort

Looking for a place to relax and explore?

Pacific Trails Resort offers cozy yurts, beautiful ocean views, and outdoor activities for different interests. Guests can spend their time hiking scenic trails, kayaking, or enjoying the peaceful views of the coast. Whether you are planning a quiet escape or an adventure, our resort has something to offer.

Why Come Visit?

Pacific Trails Resort is a great place to spend time outdoors while enjoying a comfortable stay. Guests can explore five miles of hiking trails near a state park, kayak along the coast, or start the morning with a peaceful yoga session through our Zen Retreat package.

What We Believe In

We believe spending time in nature can help people slow down and enjoy the moment. We value comfort, hospitality, and creating a welcoming place for our guests. We also want visitors to appreciate the natural beauty around them and leave with wonderful memories.

See You Soon!

We hope to welcome you to Pacific Trails Resort soon. Enjoy the beautiful coast, explore the nearby trails, and relax in nature. We look forward to helping you make wonderful memories during your stay!
